# HR notes
- Berlin entity: any change to on-call schedules requires consultation with and approval by the works council (Betriebsrat). Typical lead time 4-8 weeks.
- Berlin entity: a call-out during the night interrupts the statutory 11-hour rest period; the engineer may not start regular work until 11 hours after the call-out ends.
- US: on-call stipend $400/week primary; Germany: on-call allowance per collective policy.
- Burnout survey (Sep 2026): 7 of 9 cite night pages as the main stressor.
