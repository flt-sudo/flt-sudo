# Nordvik, Achebe & Lund (2026), working paper - cluster-randomised trial

112 teams in 9 firms (incl. 31 customer-service teams) randomised to 32-hour week vs. control for 9 months.
- Output per team (objective: tickets resolved, billable output): -2% (95% CI -7% to +3%)
- Customer-service teams subgroup: first-response time +11% slower (95% CI +4% to +18%) unless staggered schedules used
- Wellbeing (validated burnout scale): improved by 0.4 SD
- Voluntary attrition: 9.1% vs 13.0% annualised
