# Change Management Policy v4
Effective: 2026-09-01. Supersedes v3 and all team runbooks that conflict with it.

6.1 Freeze periods: no production changes except as below.
6.2 Sev-1 incidents: may deploy via Emergency CAB (minimum two approvers, one of whom is VP Engineering or delegate). Post-implementation review within 2 business days.
6.3 Sev-2 and lower: deferred until the freeze ends, unless a written freeze exception is granted by the CAB chair AND VP Engineering.
6.4 On-call leads may approve rollbacks only, not forward fixes.
