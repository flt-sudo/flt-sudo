# Treasury Policy v3 (board-approved 2025-04-22; supersedes v1 and v2)
4.1 Hedge 75% of each confirmed foreign-currency receivable above USD 1M equivalent with maturity of 12 months or less, using forward contracts.
4.2 Hedge ratios are not to be varied based on management's views of future exchange rates.
4.3 Deviations require Audit Committee approval.
